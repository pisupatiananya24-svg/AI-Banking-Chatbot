PROJECT DATASET AND RELATED LINKS

This prototype does not use an external dataset at runtime. Its sample FAQ entries, customer profile and transaction records are created locally by app.py.

Optional public datasets for future intent-classification experiments:
1. Banking77: https://huggingface.co/datasets/PolyAI/banking77
2. CLINC150: https://huggingface.co/datasets/clinc/clinc_oos

These are public intent-classification datasets, not real bank account/customer datasets.
